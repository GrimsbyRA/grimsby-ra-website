Welfare & support

Refereeing should be a positive experience. If something is worrying you, you don't have to deal with it on your own.

💚

Meet Ashley Cooper, our Welfare Officer

Ashley is here to listen and help referees find the right support if they have a concern about their wellbeing, safety, treatment or experience in football. You can get in touch to talk through a concern, ask for guidance or find out what to do next.

You don't need to know exactly what to say or have all the answers before reaching out. Share what you're comfortable sharing and Ashley can help signpost you to the appropriate support.

Contact Ashley:✉ Welfare@grimsbyra.co.ukEmail the Welfare Officer →

If someone is in immediate danger, contact emergency services. Welfare support is not a substitute for emergency assistance.
